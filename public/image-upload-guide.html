📸 How to Upload Product Images

  1. Login to Dashboard: Login Here
  2. Go to Product Form: Create New Product Edit Existing Product
  3. Find Image Upload Sections:

    📸 Main Product Image

    Primary image that appears in product lists and as the main photo

    Location: "Product Image" field in the form

    🖼️ Product Gallery (Optional)

    Additional images for product detail page gallery

    Location: "Additional Images" field in the form

  4. Upload Process:
    • Click "Choose File" button
    • Select image from your computer
    • Image will be validated automatically
    • Save the product to upload the image

📏 Image Requirements

  • File Types: JPEG, PNG, JPG, GIF only
  • File Size: Maximum 2MB per image
  • Recommended Size: 800x800px for best quality
  • Aspect Ratio: Square (1:1) works best for product grids
  • Quality: High resolution for zoom functionality
